Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

Bridgepoint Lending ("we", "us") provides business-purpose loans on investment real estate. This policy explains what we collect when you request a quote on our website, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • What you enter in our forms: your name, phone number, email, and details about the property and loan you want (such as price, rent, rehab budget, credit range and experience).
  • Basic visit information such as which ad or link brought you here.

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote and contact you about your loan request by phone, text and email, including with automated technology and AI-assisted messages, if you agreed on the form.
  • To process and service a loan if you choose to move forward, which requires additional information collected securely at that time.
  • To improve our service and advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, messaging, email and software providers), with lenders, title, insurance and other parties needed to process a loan you request, and when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your phone number or text consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Texts and calls

By checking the consent box you agreed to be contacted at the number and email you provided. Consent is not a condition of getting a loan. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, or tell us to stop contacting you at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you gave us, or to stop contacting you, by texting or calling (850) 279-8588. We keep records as the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Business-purpose loans only

Our loans are for business and investment purposes, not personal, family or household use.
