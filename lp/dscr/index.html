Common questions

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR loan qualifies you on the property's rental income compared to its mortgage payment, instead of your personal income. That's why it doesn't need tax returns or pay stubs.

Will getting a quote hurt my credit?

No. We only ask for a credit range to start. A credit check comes later, and only with your OK.

Is this for a home I'll live in?

No. These are business-purpose loans for investment properties only.

Where do you lend?

We lend on investment properties across most of the U.S. Send us your property and we'll confirm quickly.

How fast can I close?

Many deals close in a few weeks. Your loan officer will give you a realistic timeline for your specific deal.
