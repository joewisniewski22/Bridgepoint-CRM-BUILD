Where we lend and what we don’t finance

  • Not lending in: NV, ND, SD, VT, UT, OR
  • Baltimore City, MD: not lending right now
  • Some programs exclude Philadelphia, Detroit, Cleveland, Indianapolis and certain declining-market zips; we check every address
  • Rural: rentals only, at about 65%
  • Not financed: owner-occupied or primary homes, consumer-purpose loans, vacant land with no construction plan, mid-construction ground-up, hotels
  • Mixed-use is a rental loan only (no flip, bridge or ground-up)
  • Declining markets: about 5% less leverage

How to submit a deal

Send us the scenario and we’ll quote it. The fastest way to a real answer is the basics below. We come back with leverage, terms and a clear yes, no or “here’s what would make it work.”
  • Property address and loan type (flip, bridge, ground-up, rental)
  • Purchase price, and rehab budget and ARV for flips and ground-up
  • Monthly rent for rentals
  • Borrower credit score, citizenship and entity type
  • Number of completed flips or builds in the last 36 months
  • For refinances: current value, payoff and date acquired
  • Any credit events in the last 4 years
  • Appraisal to transfer, if there is one
