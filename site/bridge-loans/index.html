What is a bridge loan?

A bridge loan is short-term real estate financing that “bridges” you from where you are to where you are going: buying before you can qualify for long-term financing, closing fast on a deal that will not wait, or holding a property while you stabilize it.

When investors use bridge loans

  • Closing quickly on a purchase that traditional financing cannot match on timing
  • Buying a property that needs work or lease-up before it qualifies for long-term financing
  • Refinancing out of an existing short-term loan that is coming due
  • Moving between properties when the sale of one funds the next

The exit matters

Because a bridge loan is temporary, lenders focus on how you will pay it off: selling the property or refinancing into long-term financing like a DSCR loan. Having a clear exit plan helps your file.

What we look at

  • The property, its value and your equity or down payment
  • Your exit strategy and timeline
  • Credit and experience

Common questions

How is a bridge loan different from a fix and flip loan?

They are similar short-term loans. Fix and flip loans are built around a renovation with a rehab budget. Bridge loans are broader and are often used when the property does not need major work. A loan officer will match you to the right structure.

How long is a bridge loan?

Terms are short and depend on the deal and your exit plan. Your loan officer will confirm the term that fits your timeline.

Can I use a bridge loan on a rental property?

Yes, often as a way to acquire or reposition it before refinancing into a long-term rental loan.
