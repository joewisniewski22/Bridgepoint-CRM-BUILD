How commercial and multifamily financing works

Larger investment property is underwritten on the property itself: its income, its condition, its location and your plan for it. Terms vary more from deal to deal than with single-family rentals, which is why we start with a conversation about your specific property.

What to have ready

  • The property address and type (multifamily, mixed-use, retail, office and so on)
  • Purchase price or current value, and the loan amount you are looking for
  • Current rents or income, if the property is operating
  • Your plan: purchase, refinance, cash-out, or value-add with renovation
  • Your entity and a short summary of your experience

Mixed-use and small multifamily

Properties with apartments above retail, or buildings with a handful of units, are common investment targets. If the property is a rental that is mostly residential, a DSCR loan may fit. If it needs work first, a fix and flip or bridge loan can get you in.
