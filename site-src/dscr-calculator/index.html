How to read your DSCR

  • 1.25 or higher: strong coverage that works for most programs.
  • 1.00 – 1.24: the rent covers the payment; many programs accept it.
  • Below 1.00: the rent does not cover the payment. Some programs allow it with less leverage or other tradeoffs.
