About BridgePoint Lending

A private lender built for real estate investors who value speed, straight answers and a loan officer who picks up the phone.

What we do

BridgePoint Lending provides business-purpose financing for real estate investors: fix and flip loans, bridge loans, ground-up construction loans, DSCR rental loans, portfolio loans and commercial financing. Every loan we make is secured by investment real estate and made for a business or investment purpose — never for personal, family or household use.

How we work

  • Deal-first underwriting. We look at the property and the plan, not just your pay stubs.
  • One loan officer. You deal with a person who knows your file from first call to closing.
  • Modern tools. Estimate your deal online, apply online and upload documents securely.
  • Straight answers. We tell you what we can and cannot do early, so you can move on if it is not a fit.
