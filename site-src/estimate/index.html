How the estimate works

The estimator checks current pricing for the kind of loan you choose and shows a range, not a quote. Your actual rate depends on your credit, the property, the loan-to-value, your experience and market conditions at the time you lock. A loan officer will confirm exact numbers and itemize the fees for your deal.
