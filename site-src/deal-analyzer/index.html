Free deal analyzer

The numbers a lender and a professional analyst would run: profit, return on your cash, financed vs. all-cash, and what happens when things go wrong. Download it as a PDF.

Illustration only, based on the numbers you enter and the assumptions shown. Not an offer, rate lock or commitment to lend. Business-purpose loans on investment property only.

How the analysis works

Pick your deal type, enter the numbers, and the analyzer compares what you would earn paying all cash against what you would earn using financing. It shows your cash required, net profit, return on your cash, how many similar deals the same money could fund, and a stress test: lower value, cost overruns, a longer timeline.

The interest rate comes from our live ballpark estimator for your state and profile, so the financing side uses real market numbers, not a guess. You can change any assumption.

  • Fix & flip: purchase, rehab, ARV, hold time, selling costs
  • Rental (DSCR): cash flow, DSCR, cash-on-cash and 5-year return
  • Bridge: value created, cost of financing, and cash back if you refinance
  • Ground-up construction: draws, interest on funds outstanding, profit and break-even
