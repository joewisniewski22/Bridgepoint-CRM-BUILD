What investors say

Common questions

What types of loans do you offer?

Fix and flip loans, bridge loans, ground-up construction loans, DSCR rental loans, portfolio (blanket) loans, and financing for multifamily, mixed-use and commercial investment property. All are business-purpose loans on investment real estate.

How do fix and flip loans work?

A fix and flip loan funds the purchase and the renovation. Rehab money is held and released in draws as work is completed, and you repay by selling or refinancing. Learn more about fix and flip loans.

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR (debt service coverage ratio) loan is long-term rental financing that qualifies you on the property’s rent compared with its monthly payment, instead of your personal income. Learn more about DSCR loans.

Do you lend to LLCs and other entities?

Yes. Most investor loans close in an LLC or similar entity, and your loan officer will walk you through the entity documents.

Is this for a home I will live in?

No. BridgePoint makes business-purpose loans on investment properties only — not personal, family or household loans.

How fast can you close?

Speed depends on the loan type, the property and how quickly documents come in. Your loan officer will give you a realistic timeline for your specific deal at the start.

Does getting an estimate affect my credit?

No. The estimator asks for a credit range only. A credit check happens later, with your permission.
