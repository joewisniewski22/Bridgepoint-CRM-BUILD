Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

BridgePoint Lending (“we”, “us”) provides business-purpose loans on investment real estate. This policy explains what we collect on this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • What you enter in our forms and estimator: your name, phone number, email, and details about the property and loan you want (such as price, rent, rehab budget, state, credit range and experience).
  • Basic visit information such as which page or ad brought you here, and technical data like browser type.

How we use it

  • To show you an estimate, prepare your quote and contact you about your loan request by phone, text and email — including with automated technology and AI-assisted messages — if you agreed on the form.
  • To process and service a loan if you choose to move forward, which requires additional information collected securely at that time.
  • To improve our service, website and advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example messaging, email, hosting and software providers), with lenders, title, insurance and other parties needed to process a loan you request, and when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your phone number or text consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Texts, calls and email

By checking the consent box on a form you agreed to be contacted at the number and email you provided. Consent is not a condition of getting a loan. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, or tell us at any time to stop contacting you.

Cookies and advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies, including advertising and analytics tools, to understand how people use our site and to measure our advertising.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you gave us, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@bplending.com or calling or texting (850) 279-8588. We keep records as the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Business-purpose loans only

Our loans are for business and investment purposes, not personal, family or household use.
